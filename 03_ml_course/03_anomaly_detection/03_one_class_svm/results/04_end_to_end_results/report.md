# One-Class SVM on Penguins

Training uses 60%, threshold calibration 20%, final inspection 20%. The model wraps an RBF kernel boundary around the standardised training penguins with nu = 0.050000 and gamma = 1/p; 20 support vectors define the boundary. A held-out bird's score is rho minus its decision value: negative means inside the learned region, zero is the boundary, positive means outside. Unlike Isolation Forest (random splits) and LOF (density ratios), the boundary is smooth and deterministic, and only the support vectors are stored. The 95th percentile validation threshold is a review-budget assumption, not an estimated prevalence. Species labels are not anomaly ground truth; precision/recall cannot be claimed here.
