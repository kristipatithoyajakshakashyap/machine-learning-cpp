# Cluster experiment

Selected configuration: eps=0.700000, min_samples=12.

Silhouette assesses separation in the standardized feature space; it does not prove biological species recovery. External species labels were excluded from fitting and selection. ARI is descriptive external evaluation after selection. Noise is excluded from silhouette and its coverage is reported. The first two-feature projection can hide separation in other dimensions. Examine cluster profiles in original units and negative silhouettes before assigning meanings to clusters. These full-dataset exploratory results are not hold-out prediction estimates.
