# Exercises

1. Derive the worked fixture in math_intuition.md and compare it with the executable.
2. Add a duplicate, a constant feature and an extreme outlier; explain changes before running.
3. Compare raw and standardized units and interpret why the result changes.
4. Vary the configuration over a documented grid; save per-row diagnostics and explain why the best average may hide weak groups.
5. Reload the fitted state and verify outputs; document unsupported inference operations.

Acceptance: show equations, executable changes, saved results, and an interpretation of limitations.
